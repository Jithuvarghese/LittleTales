## Illustrations
- All characters (Pip, Bumble, Luna, Zig, Coco), objects, backgrounds and stickers are original vector art made for LittleTales, drawn as code in `src/art/`. Copyright the LittleTales project.

## Music
Built-in music is synthesised in the browser from note data (`src/services/synth.ts`). The melodies are public domain:
- "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman" (French folk tune, published 1761), the tune of Twinkle Twinkle and the alphabet song.
- Johannes Brahms, "Wiegenlied" Op. 49 No. 4 (1868). Brahms died in 1897.
- "Frère Jacques" (French traditional, 18th century).
- "Row, Row, Row Your Boat" (traditional, published 1852).
- "Mary Had a Little Lamb" (tune traditional, 1830s).
- The "play" loop is an original LittleTales composition.

No third-party recordings are used yet. Any added track must be listed here with its source, author, licence and link before use. Tracks that need credit (for example CC-BY) are shown on this screen.

## Sound effects
All sound effects (chime, pop, whoosh, boing, sparkle, boop, tada, click, twinkle, splash, drum, bell) are synthesised in code. Original to LittleTales.

## Rhymes and stories
- Nursery rhymes used are public domain: Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star (Jane Taylor, 1806); Baa, Baa, Black Sheep (1744); Hickory Dickory Dock (1744); Row, Row, Row Your Boat (1852); Mary Had a Little Lamb (Sarah Josepha Hale, 1830); Hey Diddle Diddle (1765); Rain, Rain, Go Away (17th century).
- "Five Bright Stars" is an original LittleTales counting song.
- "The Ant and the Dove" is a simple retelling of one of Aesop's fables (public domain).
- All other stories are original to LittleTales.

## Narration
- Story narration, quiz questions, praise lines, letter and number clips were generated on the developer's own computer with **Kokoro-82M** (model and voices licensed Apache License 2.0, which allows commercial use and redistribution of output), using the voices af_heart, af_bella, af_sky, af_nova, bf_emma, am_michael and am_puck, then shaped and normalised with FFmpeg (LGPL/GPL tool; no FFmpeg code is shipped with the site). Licence text checked October 2026.
- Lines without a generated clip use the voice built into the visitor's own device. No speech is ever sent to an online service from the website.
- Scene sounds (birds, sea, crickets, rain, wind) and the song backing band are synthesised in code. Original to LittleTales.
- Human recordings, when added, are credited here by first name or initial with the speaker's written permission.
