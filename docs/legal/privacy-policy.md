## The short version
LittleTales does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics in the children's area.

## What stays on your device
- Stars, stickers, finished stories, letters and numbers practised, and time spent are stored in your browser's local storage, on this device only.
- If a grown-up types a child's first name for the Name game, it is stored the same way and never leaves the device.
- Settings (volumes, text size, theme, screen-time limits) are stored the same way.
- Stories, pictures and sounds are cached by the browser so the site loads faster and works offline.

You can remove all of this at any time using "Reset all progress" in the grown-ups area, or by clearing your browser's site data.

## What we do not do
- We do not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or any contact details.
- We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising IDs or third-party scripts.
- We do not load fonts, images or code from other companies' servers.
- There is no chat, no sharing and no content uploaded by users.
- There are no in-app purchases and no links out of the site in the children's area.

## Hosting
The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like any web server, the host may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service running and secure. These logs are not used to identify children.

## Children's privacy laws
LittleTales is designed with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR (including its rules for children) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code in mind. Because no personal data is collected, no parental consent flow is needed in this version. If that ever changes, we will ask for verifiable parental consent first and update this policy.
